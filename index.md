Hello Bolo – Privacy Policy
Last updated: 11 August 2026
Hello Bolo is designed to help Urdu-speaking people communicate confidently in everyday UK situations. We take privacy seriously, and the app is built so your activity stays entirely on your device.
1. No Account Required
Hello Bolo does not require you to create an account.
We do not know who you are, and we do not store any personal identity information.
2. Nothing Is Sent Anywhere
The app has no feature that sends data to a server.
What you tap, search for, or practise never leaves your phone.
3. Lessons Live Inside the App
All lessons, words, phrases, and situations are stored inside the app itself.
Nothing is downloaded or fetched after installation.
4. Your Progress Stays on Your Phone
Your learning progress is stored locally on your device.
It is never uploaded, shared, or transmitted.
5. No Third‑Party Tracking
Hello Bolo does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third‑party tracking tools.
6. Children’s Privacy
Hello Bolo is intended for general audiences.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
7. Changes to This Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy, we will change the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.
