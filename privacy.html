Hello Bolo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026 · Applies to Hello Bolo version 1.0.2 and later

Hello Bolo counts taps, not people. The app collects usage statistics so the lessons and questions can be made better. It never collects your name, your contact details, or the words you type.

Who is responsible

Hello Bolo is made by Hassan Javed in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for the information described here. You can write to hsnjvx@outlook.com at any time.

Free, with no account

Hello Bolo is free. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to, so we do not know who you are.

The app works offline

Every lesson works with no signal at all. All the situations, words and phrases arrive once, with the app, and nothing is fetched later. The only thing the app ever sends is the usage statistics described below, and no part of the app waits for them.

Your progress stays on your phone

Your settings and your progress — the lessons you have finished, the words you know, and your answers — are stored on your device and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

What the usage statistics contain

The app keeps a small record on your phone and sends it on. It covers:

  • which screens, lessons, words and questions are used;
  • whether an answer was right, wrong, or one you asked to be shown;
  • how you practise — flashcards, questions, sentence building or review;
  • how many results a search returned and which one you tapped;
  • whether you played audio, and whether you used the slower setting;
  • whether reminders are switched on, and whether one opened the app;
  • how long the app is open;
  • your app version, whether this is a phone or a tablet, and your display choices.

Why

To make the lessons and questions better — which ones people use, which are too hard, and which parts of the app nobody finds. There is no other purpose. Nothing is used to profile you or to advertise to you.

What is never collected

Your name, your email address, your phone number, or your location. And never the words you type or search for — only how many results appeared and which one you tapped.

The random ID

The statistics carry a random ID created on your phone. It identifies that copy of the app, not you: it is not linked to your name and cannot be traced back to you. It stays on your phone until you turn the statistics off or delete the app, and turning them on again creates a new one.

Where the statistics go, and for how long

They are sent securely to PostHog, which stores them in the European Union on our behalf and may use them only to help improve Hello Bolo. They are not sold, and they are not shared with anyone else.

The individual records are kept for about a month and then deleted. What remains after that is overall totals — counts that describe everybody together and cannot be traced to any one phone. We keep those totals long-term, because they are what shows whether the app is getting better over the years.

Turning the statistics off

Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Share usage statistics. Turning it off stops everything, deletes the random ID and deletes anything still waiting to be sent. The app works exactly the same either way.

No advertising, nothing sold

There is no advertising in Hello Bolo, nothing about you is ever sold, and there is no third-party code inside the app.

Reminders

The two reminders in Settings are optional and start switched off. If you turn one on, your phone asks permission once, and the reminders are then planned and delivered by that phone alone. Nothing about them is sent anywhere, and switching them off removes them.

Pronunciation audio

The voice you hear is your phone's own built-in speech. The app does not record you, does not use the microphone, and asks for no microphone permission.

The two places the app hands you over

Contact us opens your own email app with a message ready to send. If you send it, your email address and whatever you write reach us, and we use them only to answer you.

Rate Hello Bolo opens Apple's review sheet on iPhone, or the Play Store on Android. What happens there is Apple's or Google's, not ours, and we receive no personal information from it.

Apple and Google also give us anonymous, aggregate figures of their own — how many people installed the app in a country, for example. We cannot identify anyone from those.

Your rights

You have the right to be told how your information is used, to ask for a copy of it, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict how it is used, to object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form.

The quickest way to stop everything is the switch in Settings, which takes effect immediately. Because the statistics carry no name and no account, we may not be able to find your records from an email address alone — tell us what you need and we will help as far as we can. Write to hsnjvx@outlook.com.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Hello Bolo is made for adults learning English. It is not directed at children.

What this app is not

Hello Bolo teaches the English people use in everyday situations. It is not legal, medical, financial or immigration advice, and it is not connected to the NHS, any council, school, bank or government body. For advice about your own situation, speak to the organisation concerned or a qualified professional.

Changes to this policy

If any of this changes, this page and the app's App Store and Google Play privacy information will be updated before the new version is released.
